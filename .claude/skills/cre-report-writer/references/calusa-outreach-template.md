One run per week, Mondays, up to THREE Outlook drafts for the prior 7 days' top non car condo financing leads. Lane value is always 'calusa-cre'; never touch rows with Lane 'car-condo'. The drafts position Will as an outsourced capital markets and underwriting arm, a fractional CFO for the deal. Will attaches the condensed Calusa Financing Capabilities PDF manually before sending; the body references it as attached.

## Selection

From both Development Scanner tables over the last 7 days: rank by financing relevance High then Medium, then "Est. Cost" descending, [ENTITLEMENT] items favored. EXCLUDE: car condo, garage condo or vehicle storage product (SCK lane owns those); any sponsor whose Comments anywhere carry a do-not-cold-draft note; any sponsor with an existing 'calusa-cre' row in "Developer Outreach - Drafts"; any sponsor appearing in Will's Sent Items in the last 60 days. A lead needs a contact email from the record or the week's reports; one time-boxed lookup per candidate is allowed, no fabrication, and a lead with no address found is skipped for the next ranked.

## Copy rules

No em-dashes or en-dashes; ranges use "to". Bullets end with periods. One factual project-specific clause from scanner facts only; never invent scope, cost or status. Will's voice, direct, zero pressure.
